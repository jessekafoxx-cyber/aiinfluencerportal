## LESSON 2 — Find a Winning ClickBank Affiliate Offer
**Duration:** 10:04

**Embed code:**
```html

```

**What you'll learn**
How to pick a proven affiliate offer to promote on ClickBank — the world's biggest affiliate marketplace — instead of creating your own product.

**Why it matters**
You never build the product, never handle support, and never deal with refunds. Someone else already created and tested it; you just send traffic and earn commission. ClickBank has paid out over $7.3 billion to affiliates and has 5m+ users — there is proven demand and proven payouts. People run this from $500/day up to seven figures a month off a single offer.

**How to do it**
1. Go to clickbank.com → **Start here** → create your affiliate account (name, country, email, phone, password). The onboarding questions don't affect anything — answer roughly and move on.
2. Inside the dashboard, open **Affiliate Marketplace**.
3. Browse the best-performing categories: E-business & E-marketing, Health & Fitness / Supplements, Self-Help, Make Money Online. Anything AI-related is performing strongly right now.
4. Sort by **Gravity** (high to low) — gravity means other affiliates are actively making sales with it.
5. Filter for: commission **50%+**, healthy average $/conversion (top offers pay $135–$140 per sale), recurring commissions if available (nice, not essential).
6. Click **View Sales Page** on each shortlisted offer — this is where your traffic ultimately goes. Judge whether the page would convince your audience.
7. Check the offer's affiliate tools/assets page for approved assets and hooks.
8. Grab your affiliate link (hoplink) for the offer you choose.

**Notes**
Start with **Top Offers** — it's the safest bet as a beginner. Don't dismiss "boring" niches; woodworking, survival and sleep-supplement offers convert extremely well. Pick ONE offer and stay with it.

## LESSON 4 — Building Your Bridge Page
**Duration:** 9:51

**Embed code:**
```html
<iframe src="https://fast.wistia.net/embed/iframe/w3f0agt235" title="4. Building Bridge Page" allow="autoplay; fullscreen" allowtransparency="true" frameborder="0" scrolling="no" class="wistia_embed" name="wistia_embed" msallowfullscreen width="960" height="524"></iframe>
```

**What you'll learn**
How to build a simple bridge page in GoHighLevel that sits between your content/ads and the affiliate sales page.

**Why it matters**
Sending cold social traffic straight to a sales page feels salesy and converts badly. A bridge page warms the visitor, pre-sells the offer and hands them over already believing — conversions go up significantly. You can also capture emails there and own the audience.

**How to do it**
1. Click the link below this video to start a **30-day free GoHighLevel trial** (the public site only offers 14 days; this link gives 30). A card is needed but nothing is charged for 30 days, and you can cancel anytime.
2. Inside GoHighLevel: left sidebar → **Sub-accounts** → top right **Create Sub-account** → choose **Blank Snapshot** → enter any business name/address → for the account-type question put "this is my own account" → **Add Sub-account**.
3. Switch into the sub-account → go to **Sites**.
4. Open the funnel template link below this video → copy the template URL → click **Add Funnel** and select your new location as the destination. It imports in ~30–60 seconds.
5. Open the imported funnel → **Edit**.
6. Rewrite the copy for your offer (use Claude — paste your offer research and ask it to rewrite the bridge page copy).
7. Keep it simple: one clear headline echoing your video hook, a short "why listen to me" paragraph, and one big obvious CTA button.
8. Swap the CTA button link for your ClickBank affiliate link and publish.

**Notes**
Keep it deliberately minimal — no long sales letter. If you own an upgrade, you also get advertorial-style bridge templates you can use instead. Optional but recommended: add an email opt-in so you can follow up with non-buyers.

## LESSON 5 — Creating Your AI Influencer (Claude + Higgsfield)
**Duration:** 5:44

**Embed code:**
```html
<iframe src="https://fast.wistia.net/embed/iframe/eeehfkrj0i" title="5. Creating Your AI Influencer" allow="autoplay; fullscreen" allowtransparency="true" frameborder="0" scrolling="no" class="wistia_embed" name="wistia_embed" msallowfullscreen width="960" height="524"></iframe>
```

**What you'll learn**
How to build a photorealistic AI character that looks like a real person and stays consistent across every piece of content — podcast clips, selfie videos, vintage clips and more — all driven from Claude with the Higgsfield MCP.

**Why it matters**
A consistent, believable face is what makes the account feel like a real influencer rather than AI slop. Once the character is trained, you can drop them into unlimited future scenes and keep the same identity forever.

**How to do it**
1. Sign up at **higgsfield.ai** → open the **MCP** page → copy the MCP URL.
2. In Claude: **Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector** → paste the URL → name it "Higgsfield" → **Connect → Allow**. Reopen Claude and confirm it shows as connected — you now have every Higgsfield image, video and audio model available inside Claude.
3. Paste the character-brief prompt into Claude and say: *"Please fill this out for me based on my research for my [YOUR OFFER] offer."* Claude completes the full persona brief.
4. Optional but recommended: gather 3 realistic reference images from Pinterest/Google Images matching your target demographic. Aim for real and imperfect, not glossy.
5. Back in Claude, upload the reference images and run the generation prompt, specifying the model: *"Use Nano Banana Pro 4 (or ChatGPT Image 2) via the Higgsfield MCP."*
6. Review the options Claude returns. Ask for variations until one feels authentic, then pick it: *"Option 3."*
7. Claude generates additional reference shots in multiple scenarios to lock in a persistent character, then asks to start training. Reply *"Yes, please continue training."*
8. Higgsfield trains the character (~10 minutes). When done, save the persona name — you'll reference it in every later content prompt.

**Notes**
Don't chase perfection — authentic beats flawless. If you bought this offer, an AI influencer has already been generated for you: just upload it into your Higgsfield account and skip the training step. Name your persona something memorable (e.g. "midlife_bladder_persona") because you'll call it by name in Lessons 6, 7 and 8.

## LESSON 6 — Podcast Clips
**Duration:** 11:43

**Embed code:**


**What you'll learn**
How to produce podcast-style clips with your AI influencer — scripted in Claude, generated in Higgsfield, optionally rendered in HeyGen, then captioned and posted.

**Why it matters**
Podcast clips are one of the highest-performing formats: instant authority, long watch time, heavy trust, and they let you educate and give real value instead of pitching. They're also fast and cheap to make.

**How to do it**
1. Copy the podcast-script master prompt into Claude and fill the blanks: *"Make this about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE]. Use my research above/attached."*
2. Re-attach your offer research (download the earlier Claude research as PDF and upload it) so the script stays on-message.
3. Review the script and direct it like an editor. Example rewrite request: *"Good, but cut the CTA at the end. Make it more educational. Keep the data point, open with a shocking personal story hook in the first few lines, then education, then CTA. 60 seconds."*
4. Once happy, paste the video-generation prompt: *"Use Higgsfield MCP and my avatar [PERSONA NAME]"* + the approved script. Click **Always allow** when the MCP permission prompt appears.
5. Higgsfield renders the clip. Long scripts may come back in 4 parts — that's fine. Ask: *"Please generate the rest of the clip."*
6. For a studio podcast look: in Higgsfield → **Image** → Nano Banana Pro (or GPT Image 2) → grab a podcast-set reference image from Pinterest/Google → use **Character Swap** to place your avatar into the podcast set → output 16:9.
7. Alternative tool: heygen.com — create a free account, upload your avatar look, paste the script and render there instead.
8. Stitch the parts, add captions in the Instagram edit app (as shown in later lessons), then post to Instagram and TikTok.

**Notes**
Always re-attach research before scripting — accuracy is what earns trust. Voice consistency can drift across parts; just ask Claude to fix that specific clip and it re-renders instantly. Direct the AI like a client giving feedback: hook first, data second, education third.

## LESSON 8 — Vintage / Old-School Clips
**Duration:** 7:38

**Embed code:**
```html
<iframe src="https://fast.wistia.net/embed/iframe/n0mmims3xm" title="9. Vintage Clips" allow="autoplay; fullscreen" allowtransparency="true" frameborder="0" scrolling="no" class="wistia_embed" name="wistia_embed" msallowfullscreen width="960" height="524"></iframe>
```

**What you'll learn**
How to create vintage, old-school-styled AI character clips — the format currently pulling the most attention — using one master prompt in Claude plus the Higgsfield MCP.

**Why it matters**
Old-school framing (a retired nurse, a 1990s-looking set) instantly signals experience and authority, and it stands out hard in a feed full of modern content. Same reason vintage supplement ads still work. Full 7-clip batches take about 15 minutes end to end.

**How to do it**
1. Copy the vintage master prompt into Claude and add: *"Please do this for the offer and research above for my [YOUR OFFER]."*
2. Claude fills the blanks and proposes a character concept (e.g. a retired nurse). Approve it: *"Approved."*
3. Claude generates a batch of scripts. Pick one to test: *"Please use number 1."*
4. Render in **16:9** — vintage footage wasn't shot vertical, and you'll be adding text over the top, so landscape reads as more authentic.
5. Check the first clip. If happy: *"Approved, please do the rest of the video scripts"* — it generates the whole batch in one go.
6. Watch for voice inconsistency between clips. If one drifts, ask Claude to fix that clip only; it re-renders immediately.
7. Ask Claude: *"Please stitch them all together."*
8. Download, add captions in the Instagram edit app, then post.

**Notes**
Lead with a credibility-heavy first line ("I spent 32 years as a nurse, and I can tell you the thing women lie to their doctors about most…") — that's what stops the scroll. End on a follow hook ("Follow for more, because next time I'll tell you what actually helps") rather than a hard pitch. Step-by-step manual instructions are also provided below the video if you'd rather not use the master prompt.
